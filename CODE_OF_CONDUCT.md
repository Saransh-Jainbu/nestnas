# Code of conduct

NestNAS follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. In short:

## Our standard

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to self-hosting or to Go
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it
- assume good intent, and say so when something is unclear instead of guessing the worst

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's private information, and sexual attention or advances.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (the repository, its issues and discussions, and the website) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may block people who do so repeatedly. To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [Saransh-Jainbu](https://github.com/Saransh-Jainbu), through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

Security vulnerabilities are a different channel: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
